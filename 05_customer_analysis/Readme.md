# Customer Analysis

## Summary
A customer analytics project focused on customer-base growth, new versus repeat customers, loyalty sales, spend per customer, age groups, regional customer distribution, and monthly customer trends. Built during the DataMelon Labs Industry Fellowship.

**Tools:** Databricks | SQL | Power BI

## Business Problem
The business needed to understand whether customer growth was translating into stronger customer value, and how customer behavior differed across age groups and regions.

## Work Performed
- Analyzed current-year customer count and year-over-year growth
- Measured new-customer contribution and repeat rate
- Analyzed loyalty sales and spend per customer
- Compared age groups using customer count, spend, and transaction behavior
- Compared customer distribution and spend across regions
- Reviewed monthly customer growth trends
- Built a Power BI dashboard to present the results

## Key Findings

### Customer base
- **13,944 customers** in the current year, representing **+60.6% YoY growth**
- **2,453 new customers**, equal to **17.6%** of the total customer base
- **Repeat rate of 96.9%**, down 0.4 percentage points versus the previous year

### Loyalty and spend
- **Loyalty sales of INR 214.74M**, up **48.7% YoY**
- **Spend per customer of INR 15,401**, down **7.4% YoY**

### Age groups
| Age Group | Key Finding |
|-----------|-------------|
| 26-35 | Largest group: 2,120 customers (30% share), INR 18,621 spend per customer |
| 36-45 | INR 20,497 spend per customer and 16.23 transactions per customer; spend up 2.6% YoY |
| 60+ | INR 17,767 spend per customer, up 3.3% YoY |

### Regions
| Region | Customers | YoY Growth | Spend per Customer |
|--------|-----------|------------|--------------------|
| West | 3,580 | +64.7% | INR 15,702 |
| South | 2,826 | +59.8% | INR 13,472 |
| North | 2,818 | +61.4% | INR 15,960 |
| Central | - | - | INR 15,889 |
| East | - | - | INR 15,377 |

North had the highest spend per customer, followed by Central, West, East, and South.

## Business Impact
The customer base expanded strongly, while spend per customer declined. This indicates that customer-base expansion was an important contributor to sales growth, and that customer-value analysis remains important for improving the value generated from the growing base.
