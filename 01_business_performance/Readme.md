# Retail Business Performance Deep-Dive: 12-Month Rolling Analysis

## Summary
An executive-level analysis of retail business performance using a 12-month rolling perspective. The project examined sales growth, loyalty contribution, regional performance, category concentration, payment behavior, customer acquisition, and spend per customer. Built during the DataMelon Labs Industry Fellowship.

**Tools:** Databricks | SQL | Power BI

## Business Problem
The objective was to understand the drivers behind overall sales growth rather than relying only on total sales. The analysis focused on identifying where growth was coming from and whether customer acquisition and customer value were moving in the same direction.

## Work Performed
- Performed 12-month rolling business performance analysis
- Analyzed sales trends and recent growth performance
- Evaluated loyalty-program contribution to total sales
- Analyzed regional contribution and product-category concentration
- Reviewed payment-method transaction mix
- Compared customer acquisition growth with spend per customer
- Built an executive-oriented Power BI dashboard to present the results

## Key Findings

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Total sales | **INR 895.3M** |
| Recent growth | **7.7%** over the last 6 months |
| Loyalty program sales | **INR 497.3M** (**55%** of total sales), growing faster than the overall business |
| Regional contribution | North **26.5%**, Central **13.9%** |
| Category concentration | **16 product categories** contribute 80% of sales |
| Payment mix | Card and UPI together account for **93%** of transactions |
| Customer acquisition | Up **23.8%** over the last 6 months |
| Spend per customer | Down **13%** over the last 6 months |

## Business Impact
Sales growth was supported strongly by customer-base expansion, while individual customer spending declined. This highlights the need for deeper customer-value and cohort analysis to understand how acquisition can be converted into stronger customer value.
