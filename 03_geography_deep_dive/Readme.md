# Geography Deep Dive: Regional Performance Analysis

## Summary
A geographic performance analysis covering regions, cities, city tiers, store formats, customer acquisition, revenue concentration, and underperforming markets. The project was used to understand geographic growth and the relationship between location, format, and business performance. Built during the DataMelon Labs Industry Fellowship.

**Tools:** Databricks | SQL | Power BI

## Business Problem
The business needed to understand whether growth was broad-based geographically, which locations and formats were performing strongly, where underperformance existed, and how revenue concentration could affect geographic strategy.

## Work Performed
- Analyzed performance across all five regions
- Reviewed city-level and city-tier performance
- Compared Fresh, Supermarket, and Express formats
- Analyzed new-customer growth across geographies
- Identified revenue concentration among top cities
- Reviewed product and category demand patterns by geography
- Identified underperforming cities and formats
- Built a Power BI dashboard to present the results

## Key Findings

### Regional growth
- No declining markets: national growth was **20-26% YoY** across all five regions
- West and South showed **25%+** growth
