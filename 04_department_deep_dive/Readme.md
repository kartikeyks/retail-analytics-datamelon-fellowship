# Department Deep Dive: Product Performance Analysis

## Summary
A detailed product and department performance analysis covering revenue concentration, category growth and decline, customer segments, regional variation, store formats, seasonality, brands, and customer engagement. Built during the DataMelon Labs Industry Fellowship.

**Tools:** Databricks | SQL | Power BI

## Business Problem
The objective was to identify high-performing and underperforming parts of the product portfolio, understand customer and regional differences, and translate the findings into areas for portfolio, merchandising, promotion, and retention analysis.

## Work Performed
- Analyzed revenue concentration across departments
- Reviewed category-level growth and decline
- Segmented customers into Gold, Silver, Bronze, and non-loyalty behavioral groups
- Analyzed regional product variation
- Compared Hypermarket and Supermarket shopping patterns
- Reviewed seasonal patterns and peak and lean periods
- Analyzed brand leaders, challengers, and private-label opportunities
- Reviewed customer engagement, repeat behavior, and loyalty migration
- Built a Power BI dashboard to present the results

## Key Findings

### Portfolio
- The top 5 departments drove **60-70%** of total business
- Growth categories showed **30-50%+ YoY** growth
- Brand leaders held **40-60%** share in the analyzed categories

### Customers
- Repeat customers were **3-5x more valuable** than one-time customers
- Repeat rate across the analyzed engagement view ranged from **30-90%**

### Seasonality
- Festival periods in October-November showed approximately **30-40%** uplift

### Store format shopping patterns
| Format | Items per Trip | Basket Value |
|--------|----------------|--------------|
| Hypermarket | 12-15 | INR 1,200-1,500 |
| Supermarket | 5-8 | INR 400-600 |

## Business Impact
The analysis provides a framework for balancing traffic-driving categories with margin-generating categories, tailoring assortment by region and format, using seasonality in planning, and strengthening customer engagement and retention.
